Game Recreations

Recreations of games for educational purposes, programmed in C++.

Overview

    Showcase of portal mechanics I implemented, followed by showcase of other games I recreated.

Portal Gallery

Portaling
Programmed portal gun UI, portal placement using surface normals, portal rendering with view of the other side, and portal usage.
Portal Physics
Programmed player physics integration with portals, including momentum conservation and addition, and correct orientation when passing through portals.
High Energy Pellets
Programmed pellet shooting, pellet-portal interaction, and pellet-receptacle interaction, including linked door objects that open when pellets trigger it.
Super High Energy Pellets
Programmed pellet transformation into super pellets, restrictive translucent walls, and the super pellet ability to go through these walls.
Enemy Lasers
Programmed enemy lasers that will track and kill the player, as well as kill enemies targeted by it.
Damage and Death UI
Created a UI indicator arrow that points towards the direction of damage, and bloody screen edges upon death.
Enemy Death
Added additional death conditions to the enemy, including fall damage and being hit by a fellow enemy that's falling.
Pellet-Enemy Interaction
Added additional death condition to the enemy when it's hit by a pellet, and added the ability for pellets to continue over the enemy corpse.

Other Games

Mario Kart
Programmed Mario Kart-style steering, spring camera movement, lap tracking, placement tracking, checkpoints, and enemy AI.
StarFox
Programmed flying mechanics, damage meter, shooting, HUD effects, and of course... barrel rolls.
Zelda
Programmed a Legend of Zelda map read in from a json file, a sword attack mechanic, and enemy pathfinding that updates in real time as terrain is modified.
Pac-Man Ghost AI
Programmed the 4 different types of ghost AI from the original Pac-Man game, adding debug lines and markers to demonstrate their targets.
Mario
Programmed Mario map-parsing from a json file, Mario's jumping and movement physics, basic enemy AI, and a flagpole win condition.
Frogger
Created Frogger with car and log movements, car speed-up mechanics, and win/lose conditions.
