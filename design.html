Move It!

“Move It!” is a timed 2D puzzle platformer where the player is the platform, guiding an object to goals through perilous terrain.

Team Size: 5 people
Length: 3 months
My Roles: Team Captain, Level Designer, Programmer
- Created for the USC Games graduate course: "Advanced Mobile Devices and Game Consoles"
- View the Move It page for more information on the process and links to the playable build and a GDD.

Epic Spell Wars of The Battle Wizards: Annihilageddon 3 – Satanic Panic (2024)

In the 3rd installment of the Annihilageddon series, players battle each other yet again, with the twist that one of them has become Satan.

For Annihilageddon 3, I worked on early stage ideation and physical prototyping, producing a published deck-building game sold in stores and online.
